Questions before a commercial engagement.

Confirm the relationship.

Identify the contracting entity, the person's actual role and the service being offered. Do not infer a legal role from a headline.

Request relevant evidence.

Ask for comparable project examples, references and a clear explanation of which outcomes can be documented.

Read the agreement.

Review deliverables, fees, responsibilities, cancellation terms and how disagreements would be handled.

Check specific permissions.

If a service requires authorization, verify the applicable entity and permission directly with the relevant authority.
